**The shimmer effect** works by animating a gradient background across the button. The gradient is stretched to 400% width and continuously shifted, creating an illusion of light moving across polished metal.

- **Silver gradient** — cycles through `#e5e5e5 → #ffffff → #d4d4d4`
- **Shine overlay** — a diagonal white stripe sweeps on hover, mimicking light catching a brushed surface
- **Micro-interactions** — lift on hover, press on active, giving immediate tactile feedback

Unlike neon gradients, this **monochrome palette** stays professional. It draws attention through motion, not color — making it ideal for primary CTAs in serious interfaces.
